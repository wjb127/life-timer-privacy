Tempo Rounds — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026. Developer: WJB Apps (가월). Contact: wjb127@naver.com.

Timer data stays on your device

Tempo Rounds is an interval timer. It has no account system and no developer-operated server. Routine names, interval settings, language, sound/haptic preferences and completed-session counts are stored locally on your device. We do not send these records to a server. Removing the app or clearing its storage removes local records, subject to your operating system's backup settings.

Advertising and third-party processing

The Android app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information including crash and performance data, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. Tempo Rounds does not request precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. We do not integrate additional mediation providers or a separate analytics SDK.

Read Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for third-party practices. Google controls the retention of SDK data; we do not claim a fixed retention period for that data.

Your choices

Where required, a Google consent message appears before ad requests. A Privacy options control is available in the app's Privacy panel when required by Google UMP. You can change or withdraw your choices there. Android settings also allow you to reset or delete your advertising ID. The timer works without an ad loading. Ads are placed on the setup screen and at a natural transition after a completed session, never during an active countdown.

Children and health information

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. It does not collect health or fitness measurements and provides no medical advice or health outcomes. Use routines appropriate for you.

Data requests and changes

For questions, contact wjb127@naver.com. You can remove timer records by clearing app storage or uninstalling. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy tools and the in-app consent choices. Changes to this policy will be published on this page.
