Unit Tally

Compare total prices, pack sizes and quantities. Use the same currency for both products. Weight converts to price per 100 g, volume to price per 100 ml, and counts to price per item. US fl oz means US fluid ounce. Tap Save comparison to keep a result, then Saved to reopen it.

Local data

Up to 30 saved comparisons are stored on your device. No account or backend is operated for your comparison data. Clear saved comparisons or delete the app to remove them. Copy result writes the selected result to your device clipboard.

Advertising and consent

Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform provide banner advertising on the Saved screen. Depending on platform, consent and permissions, the advertising SDK may collect device/advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, advertising interactions, app interactions, diagnostics and performance data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. No precise location, contacts, microphone or camera permission is requested.

Applicable regional consent messages are shown before eligible advertising requests. Reopen Privacy choices from the menu where required. On iOS, cross-app tracking requires Apple's authorization where requested. Refusing consent, no ad fill, or being offline does not prevent comparisons or local saving.

See Google privacy policy. Advertising partners may process data in countries other than yours. This general shopping utility is not designed or marketed specifically to children. SDK disclosures and this policy will be updated when practices change.
